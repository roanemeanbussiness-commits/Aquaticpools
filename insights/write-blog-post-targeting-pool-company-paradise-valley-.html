What is the typical timeline and investment for a luxury custom pool project?

The timeline for a luxury custom pool project in the Phoenix area can vary significantly, typically ranging from 3 to 6 months from initial design to first swim. This includes the design phase (2-4 weeks), permitting (4-8 weeks, depending on the municipality like Paradise Valley or Gilbert), and actual construction (8-12 weeks). Factors like the complexity of the design, specific features (grotto, infinity edge, detached spa), and material availability can influence these durations.

Regarding investment, a luxury custom pool by Aquatic Pool & Spa begins around $40,000 for our minimum project scope. However, for the truly custom, resort-style designs frequently requested in areas like Paradise Valley or Scottsdale, projects often range from $70,000 to well over $150,000, especially with extensive decking, landscaping, and advanced smart home integration. The final cost depends on factors such as pool size, depth, chosen finishes (e.g., specific PebbleTec colors, high-end tile, natural stone), water and fire features, automation systems, and surrounding hardscaping. We provide a transparent breakdown and detailed estimate during the design consultation.
