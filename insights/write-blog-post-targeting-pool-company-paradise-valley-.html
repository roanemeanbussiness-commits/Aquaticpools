What is the typical timeline and investment for a custom pool in Paradise Valley?

The timeline for a custom luxury pool in Paradise Valley typically ranges from 12 to 20 weeks, though this can vary depending on the complexity of the design, permitting processes, and material availability. The initial design phase, including 3D renderings and material selections, can take 2-4 weeks. Once permits are secured, excavation and structural gunite work usually take 2-3 weeks, followed by plumbing, electrical, and decking for another 4-6 weeks. The final stages, including plaster, water fill, and equipment startup, typically conclude in 1-2 weeks. Aquatic Pool & Spa prides itself on efficient project management, aiming to keep your project on track and within projected timelines.

In terms of investment, our custom luxury resort-style pools begin at a minimum project cost of $40,000. However, most elaborate designs, incorporating extensive water features, custom spas, premium decking materials, smart automation, and integrated outdoor living elements, typically range from $75,000 to well over $150,000. Factors that influence the final investment include the pool's size and shape, choice of interior finish (e.g., standard plaster vs. PebbleTec), decking materials (e.g., travertine, pavers), and the complexity of additional features like baja shelves, built-in seating, or custom lighting. We provide transparent, detailed proposals during the design phase to ensure you understand all costs involved.
