How long does it take to build a custom pool?

The timeline for building a custom luxury pool in the Phoenix area typically ranges from 12 to 20 weeks, depending on factors such as design complexity, permitting, weather, and material availability. The process usually involves design and approvals (4-6 weeks), excavation and shell construction (2-4 weeks), plumbing and electrical (1-2 weeks), decking and coping (2-4 weeks), and finally, interior finish and startup (1-2 weeks). Aquatic Pool and Spa works diligently to keep projects on schedule while maintaining our high standards of quality and craftsmanship.
