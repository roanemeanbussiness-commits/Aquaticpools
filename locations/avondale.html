Our Avondale Pool Building Process

Your Dream Pool from Concept to Creation

Six steps guide your journey to a stunning Aquatic Pool & Spa masterpiece.

01

Consultation & Design

We meet with you to understand your vision, lifestyle, and Avondale property. Our team creates a custom 3D design, ensuring every detail meets your expectations.

02

Permitting

We handle the necessary permits with Avondale's local authorities, navigating the process efficiently to keep your project on track.

03

Excavation & Steel

Our expert crews precisely excavate your site and lay the structural steel framework, forming the foundation of your custom pool.

04

Gunite & Plumbing

The shell is formed with high-strength gunite, and all plumbing for water features and filtration is meticulously installed.

05

Finishes & Decking

Choose from premium finishes like PebbleTec and custom tile. We also install beautiful, durable decking to complete your outdoor living space.

06

Fill, Start-up & Handover

We fill your pool, balance the water, and ensure all systems are running perfectly. Then, it's time for you to dive in and enjoy!
