Understanding Your Investment

Key Factors Shaping Your Custom Pool's Price

A custom pool is a unique creation, and its cost is influenced by several critical elements that reflect your vision and lifestyle.

01

Design Complexity & Size

The shape, depth, and overall footprint of your pool are primary cost drivers. Freeform designs, geometric masterpieces, vanishing edge pools, or those with elevated spas and multiple levels require more intricate engineering and construction. Larger pools naturally use more materials and labor.

02

Material Selection

From the interior finish to the surrounding decking, your material choices significantly impact the final price. Options like PebbleTec, hydrazzo, or custom tile finishes offer durability and aesthetic variety. Decking materials such as travertine, pavers, or stamped concrete also vary widely in cost and appearance.

03

Luxury Features & Technology

This is where your backyard truly becomes a personal resort. Water features like waterfalls, laminar jets, or grottoes; fire features such as bowls or pits; built-in spas; smart home automation for lighting and temperature control; and even slides or swim-up bars all add to the overall investment, creating a bespoke experience.

04

Site Conditions & Prep

The existing conditions of your backyard play a role. Factors like challenging access for heavy equipment, significant grading requirements, rocky soil, or the need to remove existing structures can add to the project's complexity and cost. Permitting and engineering requirements also contribute to the initial outlay.
