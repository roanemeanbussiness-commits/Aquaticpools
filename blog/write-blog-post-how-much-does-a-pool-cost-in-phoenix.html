What Drives the Price?

Key Factors Influencing Your Custom Pool Investment

01

Size, Shape & Depth

Larger pools naturally require more materials and labor. Complex, custom shapes like freeform, L-shaped, or negative edge designs, along with varying depths and tanning ledges, increase both design and construction complexity, directly impacting the final cost.

  • Larger square footage
  • Intricate custom shapes
  • Infinity edges & deep ends
02

Features & Enhancements

This is where your unique vision comes to life. Adding exquisite water features (waterfalls, jets, bubblers), dramatic fire features (fire pits, fire bowls), swim-up bars, integrated spas, and high-tech automation systems will elevate the luxury and the budget.

  • Custom spas & waterfalls
  • Fire features & automation
  • Lighting & smart controls
03

Materials & Finishes

The choice of coping (travertine, pavers, stone), decking (concrete, stone, wood), interior finish (PebbleTec, Quartzscape, glass bead), and tile selections (glass mosaic, natural stone) all significantly impact the final price, reflecting the level of luxury and durability.

  • High-end coping & decking
  • Premium interior finishes
  • Designer tile selections
