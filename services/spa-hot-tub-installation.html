Your Questions, Answered

Common Questions About Custom Spa & Hot Tub Projects

What types of luxury spas and hot tubs can Aquatic Pool and Spa create?

Aquatic Pool and Spa designs and installs a wide array of luxury spas and hot tubs. This includes custom gunite spas, which offer limitless design flexibility in shape and size, and can be seamlessly integrated with new or existing pools. We also craft standalone luxury hot tubs, complete with sophisticated features like waterfall spills, therapeutic jets, mood lighting, and automated controls. Our builds frequently incorporate high-end finishes such as iridescent glass tile, natural stone coping, and various PebbleTec surfaces to match your unique aesthetic.

How long does a luxury spa installation take with Aquatic Pool and Spa?

The timeline for a luxury spa installation by Aquatic Pool and Spa typically ranges from 8 to 12 weeks, depending on the complexity of the design, integration with other backyard features, and permitting processes in your specific Arizona municipality. Our meticulous design phase ensures all details are finalized before breaking ground. The build itself involves several stages: excavation, steel reinforcement, plumbing and electrical rough-in, gunite application, decking, tile and coping, equipment installation, and finally, interior finishing. We maintain transparent communication throughout each step.

Which Arizona communities does Aquatic Pool and Spa serve for spa installation?

Aquatic Pool and Spa proudly provides custom spa and hot tub installations across numerous luxury communities in the greater Phoenix area. Our service areas include Phoenix, Scottsdale, Paradise Valley, Chandler, Goodyear, Buckeye, Tempe, Mesa, Gilbert, Peoria, Glendale, Sun City, Surprise, Ahwatukee, and Avondale. We are dedicated to delivering exceptional quality to homeowners seeking sophisticated outdoor living spaces in these regions. Please note, we do NOT serve Maricopa or San Tan Valley. Our minimum project investment is $40,000, ensuring a commitment to high-end, custom builds.
