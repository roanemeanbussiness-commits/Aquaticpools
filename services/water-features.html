Your Vision, Our Craft

What Types of Water Features Can Enhance My Phoenix Pool?

Aquatic Pool & Spa specializes in a wide array of custom water features, each designed to complement your unique backyard architecture and desired aesthetic. Our expertise ensures that every element, from the subtle ripple to the dramatic cascade, is perfectly integrated into your custom pool design.

Sheer Descents & Waterfalls

These features create elegant sheets of water or natural rock cascades that spill into your pool, offering both visual tranquility and soothing sound. Perfect for modern geometric designs or rustic, naturalistic pools, they can be customized in width and flow to suit any style.

Bubblers & Deck Jets

Bubblers create playful, aerated domes of water in shallow areas, ideal for tanning ledges or steps. Deck jets shoot graceful arcs of water from the surrounding deck directly into the pool, adding a sophisticated and dynamic element to the water surface.

Fire Bowls & Water Bowls

Combining the elements of fire and water, these features create a stunning focal point. Fire bowls add warmth and dramatic ambiance, especially at night, while matching water bowls provide a gentle, overflow cascade, perfect for a balanced, high-end design.
