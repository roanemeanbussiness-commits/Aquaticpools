The Aquatic Process

How does Aquatic Pool and Spa transform an existing pool?

Our remodeling process is designed to be as personalized and stress-free as our new custom builds. We guide you through every step, ensuring your vision for a luxurious backyard retreat is perfectly realized.

01

Vision & Consultation

We begin by understanding your desires and assessing your existing pool's structure. This involves a detailed discussion about your aesthetic preferences, functional needs, and the overall style you envision for your luxury Phoenix backyard.

02

Custom 3D Design

Our design team crafts bespoke 3D renderings of your remodeled pool, allowing you to visualize every detail. This includes new shapes, water features, lighting, and material selections like PebbleTec finishes and premium tile, all custom to your property.

03

Precision Construction

Once the design is finalized, our experienced, licensed, and bonded crew brings it to life. We manage every aspect of construction, from gunite shell modifications and plumbing upgrades to the installation of new surfaces, decking, and advanced equipment.

04

Final Touches & Dive In

We meticulously oversee the final details, ensuring everything is perfect. From filling the pool to testing all new systems, we prepare your revitalized pool for its grand unveiling, ready for you to enjoy your luxurious, brand-new outdoor living space.

Remodel Possibilities

What elements can be transformed in your existing pool?

Aquatic Pool and Spa offers a comprehensive range of remodeling services, ensuring every aspect of your pool can be upgraded to luxury standards. Here are just a few ways we can enhance your Phoenix or Scottsdale pool:

Pool Resurfacing

Upgrade to modern finishes like PebbleTec, quartz, or glass beads for a durable, stunning aesthetic that lasts decades in the Arizona sun.

Tile & Coping

Select from a vast array of luxury tiles and coping materials, including natural stone, porcelain, or glass, to define your pool's edge with elegance.

Decking & Hardscape

Replace outdated concrete with premium travertine, pavers, or decorative concrete coatings to create a cohesive and luxurious outdoor living space.

Equipment Upgrades

Integrate energy-efficient pumps, filters, heaters, automated control systems, and advanced sanitization for effortless maintenance and cost savings.

Features & Lighting

Add new water features like waterfalls, laminar jets, or fire bowls. Enhance ambiance and safety with smart LED lighting solutions.

Spas & Reshaping

Integrate a new luxury spa with custom jets, or subtly reshape your existing pool's edge to enhance its flow and aesthetic appeal.
